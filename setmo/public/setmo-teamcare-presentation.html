01 · The gap

The yes is won or lost in the conversation.

  • The stall

    “I need to think about it.”

    Without a clear next step, the plan goes cold.

    A better reply“Totally fair. Which part would you like to think over?”

  • The price shop

    “I was quoted less somewhere else.”

    Value has to land before the number does.

    A better reply“Can I walk you through what's included in ours?”

  • The handoff

    “Let me talk to my spouse.”

    Your team needs a way to keep the door open.

    A better reply“Want to set a quick call so they can ask questions too?”
