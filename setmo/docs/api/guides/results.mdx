## The session

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  `created`, `in_progress`, `ended`, `completed` or `failed`. See [Sessions](/guides/sessions#lifecycle).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="failure_reason" type="string | null">
  When `status` is `failed`: `too_short`, `no_transcript`, `scoring_failed` or `abandoned`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="duration_seconds" type="integer | null">
  How long the call ran, once it has ended.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="tokens_used" type="integer">
  Tokens this call drew from your wallet (1 token = 6 seconds). `0` when the call failed with `no_transcript`, `scoring_failed` or `abandoned`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recording" type="object | null">
  `url` (an MP3) and `expires_at`. The link works for one hour; fetch the session again for a fresh one.
</ResponseField>

## The result

Present once the call is `completed`.

<ResponseField name="score" type="number">
  SetMo's overall score, 1–5 to one decimal. It's rated on the call as a whole, not averaged from the skills.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="score_pct" type="integer">
  The same score on 0–100 (`score / 5 × 100`).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="appointment_set" type="boolean | null">
  Whether the lead agreed to an appointment on this call.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="likely_to_show_pct" type="integer">
  SetMo's estimate of how likely a booked lead is to show up, based on how the call went.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="summary" type="string | null">
  One or two sentences on how the call went.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="skills" type="object[]">
  The 8 skills, each with `key`, `name`, `type` (`universal` or `call_type`), `score` (1–5), `score_pct` (0–100) and `note`. The `key`s are fixed per call type; see `GET /call-types`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="wins" type="string[]">
  What the trainee did well.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="growth" type="string[]">
  Where to grow.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="phrases" type="object[]">
  `{ you_said, try_instead }`: lines from the call with a better way to say them.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="lead" type="object | null">
  Who the trainee was really talking to: `name`, `age`, `for_whom` (when calling about someone else), `situation`, `real_reason`, `hesitation`, `tone`, `difficulty`. The trainee doesn't see the real reason or hesitation until the results.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="reveal" type="object | null">
  Whether the trainee got there. `found_real_reason` is `yes`, `partly` or `no`, and `handled_hesitation` is `yes`, `partly`, `no` or `not_raised`. Each comes with a note pointing to the moment in the call.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="transcript" type="object[]">
  The conversation turns: `speaker` (`trainee` or `lead`), `text`, `at_seconds`.
</ResponseField>
